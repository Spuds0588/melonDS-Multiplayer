Local splitscreen host — one emulated console per player on the native bridge (src/frontend/tauri/melonds_bridge.h), linked over melonDS virtual Wi-Fi. Keys 1-4 switch which console the keyboard drives; hold Shift-Right (or press Tab) to peek at that console's touch screen; the mouse is the pen on it at all times, shown or not.
